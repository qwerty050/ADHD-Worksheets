What This Project Does

This tool takes the very lectures you are currently studying from, along with your dense reference materials and past questions, and automatically forges them into highly structured, easy-to-digest worksheets. It breaks down overwhelming blocks of text into bite-sized, accessible learning modules tailored exactly to your curriculum.

Why I Built This

Traditional academic materials are often walls of text that can be incredibly hostile to neurodivergent learners. I wanted a tool that respects how neurodivergent minds process information: providing immediate structure, clear boundaries, and scaffolded questions that guide the learner step-by-step rather than dropping them into the deep end.

The Focus-Driven Edge

No Context Switching

Everything you need—theory, examples, and questions—is bundled into single modules. Say goodbye to juggling 15 open browser tabs just to finish one topic.

Zero Distractions

Clean, predictable formatting focuses entirely on the content. No cluttered sidebars, no flashing animations, just pure, structured knowledge.

Scaffolded Learning

We break overwhelming concepts into stepping stones, guiding you from basic recall up to complex application seamlessly.

Active Practice & PYQs

Instead of passively reading, apply what you learn instantly. We map previous year questions (PYQs) directly alongside the theory, so you get complete, topic-wise practice exactly how it will be tested.

Who This Is Helpful For

  • Neurodivergent Students: Anyone who gets overwhelmed by unstructured reading assignments.
  • Self-Learners: People tackling complex subjects on their own who need a guided path.
  • Educators: Teachers looking to quickly generate accessible, scaffolded practice materials.
